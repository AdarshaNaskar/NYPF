Our Story

Crafted for True
Pizza Lovers.

Born from the bustling streets of New York and brought to the heart of Kolkata, NYPF is more than just a pizza place—it's a culinary obsession.

We believe in the power of the perfect crust, the richness of premium San Marzano tomatoes, and the magic of 100% real mozzarella. Every pie is handcrafted to deliver a sensory experience that leaves you craving for more.

  • Signature Thin Crust Perfection
